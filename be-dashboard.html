The destination — what "great" means

Not bigness. These three outputs, held on three tests. Grade yourself in the Diagnostic tab.

Superior Results

You win at your chosen game: financial performance and progress toward the purpose.

Distinctive Impact

"If we disappeared tomorrow, who would miss us, and why?" A hole nothing else could easily fill.

Lasting Endurance

Prosperous beyond any leader, idea, or cycle. If it cannot be great without you, it isn't great yet.

Return on Luck — the multiplier on everything below.

You don't control luck (un-caused, consequential, surprising). Great companies get a higher return on it. Caldwell: 2,801 days before the good luck arrived.

About this dashboard

This is the interactive companion to the BE Framework & Operating System document set — built for an owner-founder running a small or mid-sized company. It works entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded; everything you enter is stored in this browser's local storage. Use Export data to keep a portable backup (JSON) and Import to restore it — for example, after clearing your browser data or moving machines.

The idea in one line

A founder with the right people, thinking clearly, acting with relentless tactical discipline — turn by turn of the flywheel — until the company outlasts the founder.
